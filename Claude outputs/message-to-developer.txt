🎨 DESIGN SPECS (Quick Reference)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Layout:
  • Horizontal scrollable sections (3 separate)
  • Responsive: Mobile swipe-enabled, Desktop scroll arrows

Colors:
  • Active button: #2D7C3F (green) + white text
  • Inactive button: #E8E8E8 (light gray) + dark text

Animations:
  • ONLY smooth transitions: 150-200ms on button click/selection
  • NO page jumps, NO slide animations, NO unnecessary effects

Buttons:
  • Touch target: 44x44px minimum
  • Padding: 10-12px horizontal, 8px vertical
  • Font: 14px medium weight
